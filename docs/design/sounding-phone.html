Sounding on a phone

A working prototype of the redesigned screen, replaying a real sounding log (synthetic-memo.gemini.jsonl, 63s, 16 interpretation calls). The board is the whole screen. Tap the round button to drop a sounding; tap it again to surface. Pull the sheet up from the bottom for the transcript and the chart; it rests at a medium and a large height. Drag the chart to replay any moment. Tap a mark to follow it.

How to read it. The board holds at most seven marks; size is prominence relative to the others on screen, not a score. A new mark rises with abuoy line that fades in a couple of seconds; the one it displaces sinks. The words you say flash and shrink within a second of saying them. When you go quiet the board dims to slack. Context, in the sheet, seeds faint dashed marks for what you already know you're circling; they only become real when your talking reaches them. Surface ends the sounding; the chart then shows the final reading ranked above the lanes.
